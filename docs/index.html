IThe First Door

You stand before an old iron door. Scratched into it is a riddle, and beneath the riddle, a message in a scrambled alphabet.

The Riddle

I am the beginning of eternity, the end of time and space, the beginning of every end, and the end of every place.
What am I?

The answer is a single letter. Its position in the alphabet (A = 1, B = 2, C = 3 …) is the key to the cipher below.

The Cipher

This is a Caesar cipher: every letter of the message has been pushed forward through the alphabet by the same number of places. Spaces and punctuation are left alone.
